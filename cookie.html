1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device to record information about your visit. They can be set by the site you are visiting (first-party cookies) or by third parties whose content is embedded in the page (third-party cookies). Depending on their purpose, they are classified as technical cookies — necessary for the site to work and, under Italian law, not requiring consent — or as profiling and analytics cookies, which do require prior consent.

2. Cookies used by this website

This website does not set any cookies on your device. It is a purely informational site: it uses no analytics, no advertising, no profiling, no social plug-ins, no tag managers and no session cookies. For this reason no cookie consent banner is displayed.

3. Third-party services

Although no cookies are set, the site loads one resource from a third-party server. Any request to an external server necessarily transmits your IP address to that server.

Service Provider Purpose Cookies set
Google Fonts Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC Delivery of the typefaces used across the site (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) None. Your IP address and browser data are transmitted to Google in order to serve the font files.

Google’s handling of this data is described in its Privacy Policy and in the Google Fonts privacy FAQ. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, the transfer relies on the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or on Standard Contractual Clauses.

Server logs kept by our hosting provider record technical browsing data as described in section 2.1 of the Privacy Policy. This is not done through cookies.

Even though this site sets no cookies, you can manage or block cookies for any website through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may prevent other websites from working correctly.
