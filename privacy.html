2. What data we process

2.1 Browsing data

The IT systems and software procedures used to operate this website acquire, in the course of their normal operation, some personal data whose transmission is implicit in the use of internet communication protocols. This includes IP addresses, browser and operating system type, request time and other parameters relating to the visitor’s technical environment. This data is used solely to deliver the website and to obtain anonymous statistical information on its use, and to check that it is functioning correctly. It is collected and retained by our hosting provider in server logs and is deleted according to that provider’s retention schedule.

2.2 Data you send us voluntarily

The website contains no contact forms. If you write to info@fraido.it or to any other address published on this site, we process the personal data contained in your message (typically name, email address and any information you choose to include) for the sole purpose of replying to you and managing the resulting relationship.

2.3 Cookies

This website does not use profiling or analytics cookies. See the Cookie Policy for full details.

4. Who has access to the data

Personal data may be accessed by staff of Fraido Srl authorised to process it, and by external suppliers acting as data processors under Article 28 GDPR — in particular the hosting provider and the email service provider. Data is not disclosed or sold to third parties for their own purposes and is not subject to automated decision-making or profiling.

Loading web fonts from Google Fonts causes your IP address to be transmitted to Google, which may involve a transfer outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers rely on the European Commission’s adequacy decision for the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or on Standard Contractual Clauses. See the Cookie Policy.

5. Your rights

Under Articles 15 to 22 GDPR you have the right to:

  • obtain confirmation of whether we process your data and access it;
  • obtain the rectification of inaccurate data and the completion of incomplete data;
  • obtain the erasure of your data, in the cases provided for by law;
  • obtain the restriction of processing;
  • receive your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format (portability);
  • object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interest;
  • withdraw any consent given, at any time, without affecting processing carried out beforehand.

To exercise these rights, write to info@fraido.it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, garanteprivacy.it) or with the supervisory authority of the EU country where you reside or work.

6. Changes to this policy

Fraido Srl may update this policy to reflect changes to the website or to applicable law. The current version is always published on this page, with its date of last update.
